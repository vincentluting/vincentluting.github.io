In 2015 I got on a plane from Beijing to Amsterdam with two suitcases and a plan. I would do a master's in Dutch linguistics at Leiden, and after two years I would go home.

That was nine years ago. This is home now.

## Why Dutch?

People always ask this, and the honest answer is a bit of an accident. <!-- TODO(Vincent): how did you end up studying Dutch at the Communication University of China? --> What started as a subject became a door. Very few people in China speak Dutch, and very few Dutch people expect a Chinese person to answer them in their own language. That surprise opened a lot of conversations.

## The first year

What I remember most is how direct everyone was. In China, you often have to read between the lines. In the Netherlands, the line is the message. It took me a while to stop looking for a hidden meaning, and a while longer to start being that direct myself.

## Staying

After Leiden, I got a job at Huawei in The Hague. Then Amazon, then Philips, then Tencent. Every job was a reason to stay a little longer, and at some point I noticed that I was no longer counting the years.

I became a Dutch citizen. <!-- TODO(Vincent): when, and what did it feel like? --> I passed the NT2 Staatsexamen. I started to have opinions about which supermarket is best and when you really need a raincoat. (Always.)

## What living between two cultures taught me about work

Most of my work has been in between: Chinese companies in Europe, and European companies working with Chinese partners. What I learned is that most misunderstandings are not about language. They are about expectations. Who decides? How direct can you be? What does "yes" mean?

Being in the middle is not always comfortable. But it is useful. I can often see a problem coming because I recognise both sides of it.

## What comes next

I have changed jobs more often than I would like. For the next one, I want to go deep and stay for years. Nine years ago I thought I was only visiting. Now I am looking for a place to put down roots, in my work as well.

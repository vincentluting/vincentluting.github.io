## The situation

In August 2023 I joined Tencent's Europe IT Center in Amsterdam. I became the IT contact for three game studios: Yager in Berlin and Tequila Works in Madrid, two studios Tencent had invested in, and LightSpeed UK, an internal Tencent studio.

Central IT had good offers for the studios: a group deal for Perforce, the version-control tool game studios use for large files, a set of security services, and cloud and software discounts. My job was to make sure the studios knew about them, could use them, and always had someone to ask.

## Helping the studios take up the tools

I held regular meetings with the studios to hear what they needed. With the Tequila Works IT manager I had a monthly check-in and sent a weekly update. I helped their IT contacts on Slack when they got stuck with Perforce or a security service. And I built three trackers myself, one of them semi-automated, so I always knew where each studio stood.

In 2023 Yager and Tequila Works took up 245 Perforce licences (172 and 73) and five security services, from security alerts to phishing simulation. When Yager took up the Perforce deal, I checked the quote and found a calculation error. Correcting it saved the studio $3,000.

## Building trust

In August 2024 I went to Gamescom in Cologne. I hosted meetings with studios, including Tequila Works, and helped the CES team with their happy hour for more than 100 external customers. Gamescom is where I earned the trust of the Tequila Works COO.

At Yager I helped the HR team with remote IT questions, which built trust with both their IT and HR people. And I aligned two roadmaps with studio leaders.

## A new IT setup for LightSpeed UK

LightSpeed UK needed its own local IT environment for game development, based on a central blueprint. I was the project manager. I agreed the blueprint and the charging model with the studio's management, set up a Jira dashboard, and gave their leadership a monthly update. Together with our infrastructure engineers, we upgraded the studio's network from 2 to 3 Gbps.

## Carrying the studios' voice

The product teams were mostly in the US. In 2024 I collected more than 11 requirements from the studios and passed them on. The security team improved more than six of their products with that input. Across the wider security adoption programme, a colleague on that team wrote that adoption grew about 40% in a year, "a success to which Lu Ting has significantly contributed".

One colleague described me as "the gateway between studio PM and product owner". I like that description. My work was to keep both sides talking, keep track of everything, and check the details before they went out.
